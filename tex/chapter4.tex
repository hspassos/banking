\chapter{Sample and Data}

The empirical model is based on \citeonline{maudosFactorsExplainingInterest2004}. It is a single stage model and the variables used are shown in Table \ref{table:1}.

\begin{sidewaystable}[h!]
    \centering
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.5} % Espaçamento entre linhas
    \begin{tabular}{ l l c }
    \hline
    Variable & Name & Financial Statement Accounts \\ [0.5ex] 
    \hline\hline
    $\textit{nim}$ & Net Income Margin & $\dfrac{\textit{Operating Revenue} - \textit{Capital Raising Cost}}{\textit{Realizable Assets}}$ \\ 
    $\textit{hhi}$ & HHI Index & $\sum_{i=1}^n{(market share_i)^2}$ \\ 
    $\textit{aoc}$ & Average Operating Costs & $\dfrac{\textit{Operating Costs}}{\textit{Total Assets}}$ \\
    $\textit{riskaver}$ & Risk Aversion & $\dfrac{\text{Total Equity}}{\textit{Total Assets}}$ \\ 
    $\textit{sd}$ & Volatility of market interest rates \\ 
    $\textit{crerisk}$ & Credit Risk & $\dfrac{\textit{provisions for credit operations}}{\textit{Total Credit Operations}}$ \\ 
    $\textit{sd*crerisk}$ & Interaction between credit risk and market risk & $sd \times crerisk$ \\ 
    $\textit{size}$ & Average size of operations & $\log_{10}(\textit{Total Credit Operations})$ \\
    $\textit{iip}$ & Implicit interest payments & $\dfrac{\textit{Operating Costs} - \textit{Services Income}}{\textit{Total Assets}}$ \\ 
    $\textit{reser}$ & Opportunity costs of bank reserves & $\dfrac{\textit{Cash and Cash Equivalents} - \textit{Interbank Liquid Investments}}{\textit{Total Assets}}$ \\ 
    $\textit{ef}$ & Implicit interest payments & $\dfrac{\textit{Operating Costs}}{\textit{Operating Revenue}}$ \\ 
    [1ex] 
    \hline
    \end{tabular}
\caption{List of variables used in the empirical model}
\label{table:1}
\end{sidewaystable}
